Well Water

Well water is obtained from underground water sources and is commonly used for drinking, household activities, and agriculture. Its quality can be influenced by soil conditions, nearby drainage, sewage leakage, and natural minerals. Contamination may introduce harmful microorganisms or increase certain chemical parameters. AquaGuard AI helps assess the quality of well water and identify potential safety concerns.

River Water

River water is an important surface-water source used for domestic, agricultural, and industrial purposes. Its quality can change due to wastewater discharge, agricultural runoff, industrial pollution, and other environmental activities. Increased turbidity, chemical contamination, and harmful microorganisms can affect its safety. AquaGuard AI helps monitor river-water quality and identify possible contamination.

Tap Water

Tap water is generally treated before it reaches households through a distribution system. However, contamination can occur because of damaged pipelines, leakage, poor storage conditions, or prolonged stagnation. Changes in physical, chemical, or microbiological parameters can affect water safety. AquaGuard AI helps users evaluate the quality of their tap water and detect potential issues.

Lake Water

Lake water is a surface-water source that can be affected by natural conditions and human activities around the lake. Agricultural runoff, wastewater, waste disposal, algae growth, and microbial contamination can reduce water quality. Changes in turbidity, pH, dissolved substances, and microorganisms may indicate pollution. AquaGuard AI helps assess lake-water quality and identify possible risks.

Industrial Water

Industrial water can be influenced by manufacturing processes, chemical use, and industrial discharge. Improperly treated wastewater may contain excessive dissolved substances, chemicals, or other contaminants that can affect surrounding water resources. Regular monitoring is therefore important to identify changes in water quality. AquaGuard AI helps analyze industrial-water parameters and highlight potential contamination risks.
