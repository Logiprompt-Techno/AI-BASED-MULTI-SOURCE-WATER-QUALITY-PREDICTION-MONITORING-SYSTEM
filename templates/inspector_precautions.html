Precaution Methods

Standard water purification methods and safety precautions.

Boiling

Bring water to a rolling boil for at least 1-3 minutes to kill pathogenic bacteria, viruses and protozoa.

Chlorination

Use liquid household chlorine bleach or purification tablets. Highly effective against bacteria but less effective against protozoa.

RO Purification

Reverse Osmosis removes chemical contaminants, heavy metals, and dissolved solids effectively.

UV Purification

Ultraviolet light deactivates the DNA of bacteria and viruses. Requires clear water to be effective.

UF Filtration

Ultrafiltration removes suspended solids, bacteria, and viruses but does not remove dissolved minerals.

Activated Carbon

Improves taste and removes chlorine, odors, and certain organic chemicals.

Sand Filtration

Slow sand filtration is a traditional biological process that effectively removes pathogens and turbidity.

Laboratory Testing

If chemical contamination is suspected (heavy metals, high nitrates), water must not be consumed until tested by a certified lab.
