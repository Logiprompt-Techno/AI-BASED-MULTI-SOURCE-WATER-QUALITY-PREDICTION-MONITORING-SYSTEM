About AquaGuard AI

The Importance of Water Quality Monitoring

Clean and safe water is essential for human health, agriculture, and the environment. Contaminated water can lead to severe health issues and ecosystem damage. Regular monitoring is crucial to detect pollution early and ensure that water sources remain safe for their intended use.

How AI Detects Pollution

Our system utilizes a state-of-the-art Random Forest Machine Learning model trained on historical water quality data. By analyzing multiple parameters such as Temperature, Dissolved Oxygen (DO), pH, Biological Oxygen Demand (BOD), and various coliform counts, the AI can detect complex patterns indicative of pollution. It predicts whether a given sample is SAFE or UNSAFE with high accuracy and determines the severity of the pollution.

Supported Water Sources

  • Well Water: Ground water that can be affected by chemical runoff.
  • River Water: Highly susceptible to agricultural and industrial discharge.
  • Tap Water: Monitored to ensure municipal supply safety.
  • Lake Water: Standing water prone to algae blooms and stagnation.
  • Industrial Water: Discharge that must be treated before environmental release.
